How a digest is chosen, step by step

Ten made-up items wait in the inbox. Choose a time to read, a focus goal and a seed. Then press "Next step": each step takes one item into the digest, or says why it does not. Change the minutes of an item, or mark it as shown in a recent digest, and the digest starts again.

    The digest so far

      Who can come. Only items of the inbox, and none that a digest showed in the last 3 weeks.

      The random slot. It is reserved first, so that it always has its place. It takes an item that no other rule chooses, of at most a quarter of the time (but at least 5 minutes, and never more than the time).

      The rules. Then the focus goal, other goals, saved again and recent, in this order. A score helps from 2 up. "You said wrong" turns a score: a wrong 3 counts as 0, a wrong 0 as 2.

      The time. An item that does not fit the minutes that are left is skipped, and a shorter item can still come. A digest has at most 8 items.

      The page uses the rules of apps/api/src/unshelve/resurface/selection.py and the random numbers of Python's random.Random (SHA-512 of the seed, then a Mersenne Twister, then random()). So a seed gives the same random pick here and in the API. Without a secure browser context (for example over plain HTTP), the page cannot compute SHA-512 and says so. Back to note 34.
